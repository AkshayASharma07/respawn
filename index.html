NANO-PULSE CLINICAL MANUAL

NANO-PULSE merges visual novel case histories with socioeconomic medical procedures across three tiers, playable across 3 difficulty tiers (Resident, Specialist, Chief Surgeon).

Siddharth (Upper-Middle Class) — Rigid Precision:
  • Stage 1 - Specular Mirrors: Law of reflection (θ_i = θ_r) off 45° planar mirrors.
  • Stage 2 - Affine Transformation: Drag to rotate and scale the blue vector glyph onto the red target outline (Press ENTER or Space to commit matrix congruence).
  • Stage 3 - Geodesic Routing: Multi-bounce path through crystalline neural barriers.
Kabir (Middle Class) — Bio-Cleanse Laser Protocols:
  • Stage 1 - Wealthy Patient Protocol: Precision laser scalpel aiming & micro-vessel purge (Dissolve 10 pathogens).
  • Stage 2 - Middle-Class Protocol: Resource strain with accelerated energy depletion, sluggish recharge, and heavily armored purple strains (Purge 15 pathogens).
  • Stage 3 - Lower-Class Protocol: Extreme resource starvation, brutal overheat lockout, and elimination of the colossal Apex Boss Mutation.
Radha (Low-Income Class) — Fragile Margin:
  • Stage 1 - Micro-Needle Slits: Extreme sub-degree aperture threading between toxic decay plates.
  • Stage 2 - Delaunay Triangulation: Click three nodes to span non-overlapping triangles and reconstruct necrotic tissue.
  • Stage 3 - Compound Ricochets: Minimum required ricochet count before striking final receptive node.
Difficulty Levels:
  • Resident: Full 85-step trajectory guide line, standard speed, generous tolerance.
  • Specialist: Short 28-step trajectory guide, +40% speed, smaller target margins.
  • Chief Surgeon: Blind aiming (0 guide line), +80% speed, pinpoint precision required.
